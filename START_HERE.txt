DRAFT WAR ROOM
==============

1. Double-click  draft-war-room.html   (opens in your browser)
2. Go to the DATA tab
3. Upload         war_room_import.csv
4. Go to the LEAGUE tab and set up both of your leagues
5. Export a backup from the Data tab

That is the whole setup. The tool works offline and saves to your browser.

To regenerate projections later:
    pip install -r requirements.txt
    ./run_all.sh

Read README.md for everything else.
